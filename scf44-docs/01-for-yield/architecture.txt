01 Stellar Integration Plan
1.1 Integration Architecture
ForYield integrates seven building blocks from the official SCF Integration List. Each one removes a
real barrier to bringing regulated European capital onto Soroban.
Technical Architecture Doc github.com/Foryield/soroban-yield-vault (architecture, contract interface,
demo walkthrough). Provided alongside this document.
DeFindex Yield infrastructure layer and allocator routing. The vault allocates capital
across whitelisted Soroban yield strategies via DeFindex rather than
building strategy routing from scratch. (Tranche 2.)
Blend v2 Soroban lending primitive. The primary yield venue for stablecoin supply
positions, accessed through the DeFindex allocator.
Aquarius AMM and liquidity layer (~$40M TVL). Provides LP yield strategies and
swap liquidity for vault rebalancing.
Soroswap Soroban-native DEX aggregator. Powers client-facing crypto-to-crypto
exchange and internal rebalancing across the full Stellar DEX stack
(Phoenix, Aquarius, SDEX).
Allbridge Cross-chain bridge. Lets EVM and Solana users fund the vault on Stellar
without manual bridging secondary onboarding path; primary
onboarding is EUR via the regulated fiat ramp.
DFNS Wallets-as-a-Service. Provisions Soroban-compatible embedded wallets
from an email login for non-crypto-native patrimonial clients. DFNS
integration.
Stellar Wallets Kit Wallet connection layer for crypto-native users (Freighter, Albedo, xBull).
Live in the testnet vault UI today.
Supporting institutional infrastructure (not Integration Track scope, not funded by this grant): Fireblocks MPC
custody for segregated AUM (required under MiCA), Elliptic or Notabene for Travel Rule and KYT screening, and
Certora for the Soroban smart contract audit (covered by the Stellar LaunchKit audit credit at Tranche 2).

1.3 Why Stellar
First EURC and regulated settlement. Stellar is the only L1 where Visa has activated EURC
settlement (2025). For a MiCA-regulated product denominating client positions in EUR, EURC on
Stellar removes FX volatility and matches the AMF's expectation of regulated stablecoin rails. No
other chain oﬀers this regulatory ﬁt for a European yield product.
Second economics that make regulated yield viable. Soroban settles in under ﬁve seconds
at sub-cent fees. At our ticket sizes this is the diﬀerence between a workable and an unworkable
cost structure for a managed yield product. The same vault logic on EVM would be prohibitively
expensive at retail scale.
Third production-ready ecosystem. DeFindex, Soroswap, Aquarius, Blend, DFNS and Stellar
Wallets Kit provide infrastructure that would take months to build from scratch. We focus on the
regulated vault, compliance reporting and distribution, and leverage battle-tested Stellar
integrations for allocation, liquidity, swaps and wallets.
