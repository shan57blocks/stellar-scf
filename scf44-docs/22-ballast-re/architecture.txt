baUSD	Technical	Architecture
A	Soroban-native	vault	for	real-world	reinsurance	yield
This	document	specifies	the	complete	technical	architecture	of	baUSD.	The	architecture	is	finalized	at	time	of	application;	Tranche	1	is	development,	not
design.	The	protocol	is	built	end-to-end	on	the	Stellar	stack	and	composes	with	existing	ecosystem	building	blocks	for	distribution,	wallet	access,	liquidity,	and
cross-chain	inflow.
1
Overview
baUSD	is	a	yield-bearing	token	issued	natively	on	Soroban.	An	allowlisted	institutional	LP	deposits	USDC	on	Stellar	into	a	vault	contract	and	mints	baUSD,	an
appreciating	share	token	whose	value	rises	as	earned	premium	from	regulated	US	casualty	specialty	reinsurance	treaties	accrues.	Yield	is	uncorrelated	to	crypto,
equities,	and	interest	rates	because	it	is	sourced	from	insurance	underwriting	profit,	not	token	emissions,	lending	spread,	or	duration.
The	defining	design	constraint	is	that	
deposited	capital	does	not	remain	in	the	contract
.	The	majority	flows	off-chain	into	reinsurance	treaties	through	a
Bermuda	Class	3A	Segregated	Accounts	Company	(SAC)	and	is	collateralized	in	Reg	114	Trusts.	Consequently	the	vault	holds	only	a	liquidity	sleeve	for	near-term
redemptions,	the	on-chain	
total_assets
	is	an	attested	NAV	rather	than	a	balance	of	held	USDC,	and	redemptions	are	gated	by	an	allowlist	and	a	notice	period
because	casualty	tail	development	means	capital	cannot	be	returned	on	demand	at	full	value.	This	is	what	separates	baUSD	from	a	synthetic	or	wrapped
representation:	the	share	token	tracks	attested	treaty	performance,	including	downside.
2
Stellar	Relevance	—	Core,	Not	Superficial
Stellar	is	not	used	for	storage	or	as	a	thin	wrapper.	The	core	financial	logic,	share	accounting,	mint/burn,	NAV	accrual,	and	redemption,	executes	inside	Soroban
smart	contracts.	baUSD	exists	only	as	a	Stellar	asset,	its	yield-bearing	behavior	is	enforced	on-chain,	and	its	distribution	runs	through	Stellar-native	yield
infrastructure.	Remove	Stellar	and	there	is	no	product.	The	ecosystem	building	blocks	in	section	6	are	leverage	on	top	of	this	protocol,	not	the	protocol	itself.

baUSD	is	implemented	as	a	SEP-41-compatible	token	contract.	Mint	and	burn	authority	is	held	exclusively	by	the	vault	contract	(set	as	token	admin);	there	is	no

3.2		Vault	contract	(appreciating-share	/	ERC-4626-equivalent)
The	vault	is	the	core	logic	contract.	baUSD	shares	represent	a	pro-rata	claim	on	the	attested	NAV;	share	value	=	total	assets	/	total	shares.	There	is	no	rebasing,
balances	are	constant	and	value-per-share	moves.

assets_returned	=	shares_burned	*	total_assets	/	total_shares			
//	at	redemption
share_price					=	total_assets	/	total_shares																			
//	NAV	per	baUSD

3.3		Allowlist	/	compliance	module
A	module	maintains	the	set	of	KYC-verified	institutional	addresses.	
subscribe
	and	
request_redemption
	revert	for	non-allowlisted	callers,	and	baUSD	
transfer
checks	both	sender	and	recipient,	preventing	secondary	distribution	to	unverified	parties.	The	compliance	authority	adds/removes	addresses	based	on	off-chain	KYC
completion.
3.4		NAV	/	yield-accrual	module
An	authorized	attestation	service	raises	(or	lowers)	on-chain	NAV	from	off-chain	attested	earned-premium	and	reserve	data,	driving	baUSD	appreciation.	The	update
path	is	constrained	on-chain	(section	7.2)	so	the	largest	trust	assumption	in	the	system	has	a	bounded	blast	radius.

5
Yield	Mechanism
baUSD	uses	an	appreciating-share	model,	not	rebasing:	total	vault	assets	grow	as	premium	is	earned,	so	each	baUSD	redeems	for	more	USDC	over	time.	The	6%+
APY	target	is	net	earned	reinsurance	premium,	not	token	emissions,	lending	spread,	or	T-bill	duration,	which	is	the	source	of	its	non-correlation	to	crypto	and	rates.

Primary	distribution	—	DeFindex	(funded)
DeFindex	is	Stellar's	yield	infrastructure	for	wallets	and	DeFi	apps.	baUSD	is	implemented	as	a	DeFindex	Strategy	conforming	to	the	strategy	interface,	validated
against	the	DeFindex	Quickstart	and	API	Reference,	with	developer	support	via	the	Palta	Labs	Discord.	This	turns	baUSD	from	a	standalone	vault	into	wallet-

vault's	standard	entrypoints.
Wallet	access	(funded)
The	dApp	uses	Stellar	Wallets	Kit	for	a	unified	multi-wallet	connect	flow	and	supports	Freighter	Connect	as	the	primary	Soroban	browser	wallet	for	direct	vault

Lending,	liquidity,	cross-chain	(roadmap)
Post-mainnet,	baUSD	is	onboarded	as	Blend	v2	collateral	(borrow	against	the	position	without	exiting),	paired	on	Soroswap	with	Aquarius	market	data	for	secondary

listed	building	block.

7.2		NAV	attestation	constraints
The	attested	NAV	is	the	single	largest	trust	assumption,	constrained	on-chain	to	limit	blast	radius:
Bounded	delta:
	
update_nav
	reverts	if	the	change	exceeds	a	configurable	per-update	cap,	forcing	large	moves	to	be	staged	and	visible.
Multisig:
	attestation	authority	is	an	m-of-n	multisig,	not	a	single	key.
Freshness:
	updates	carry	a	timestamp;	consumers	and	Reflector	can	treat	NAV	beyond	a	max	age	as	stale/invalid.
Proof	reference:
	each	update	logs	a	
proof_ref
	(hash/URI	of	the	supporting	earned-premium	and	reserve	attestation)	for	auditability.
Monotonicity	not	assumed:
	NAV	can	decrease.	Downward	updates	are	explicitly	permitted	so	the	token	reflects	real	losses	rather	than	masking	them.
7.3		Other	controls
Smart-contract	security:
	independent	audit	via	the	SCF	Audit	Bank	(not	in	budget),	with	remediation	before	mainnet.
Access	control:
	vault-bound	mint/burn	authority;	allowlist-gated	subscription	and	redemption;	Soroban	
require_auth
	on	all	privileged	calls.
Reentrancy:
	checks-effects-interactions	ordering;	state	updated	before	external	token	transfers.
Pausability:
	guardian	can	pause	subscribe/redeem	in	an	incident	without	touching	balances.
Upgradeability:
	Soroban	contract	upgrade	gated	behind	the	governance	multisig	and	a	configurable	timelock,	giving	holders	advance	notice	of	code	changes.
Liquidity	risk:
	the	redemption	notice	period	and	sleeve	sizing	are	the	buffer	against	a	redemption	rush	exceeding	onchain	liquidity;	excess	requests	queue	until
the	sleeve	is	refunded.
7.4		Off-chain	trust	assumptions	(explicit)
Disclosed	rather	than	engineered	away:	accuracy	of	earned-premium	and	reserve	data	feeding	attestation	(mitigated	by	independent	actuarial	review);	custody	and
movement	of	off-chain	capital	by	treasury	ops;	solvency	and	claims-paying	behavior	of	the	SAC,	backstopped	by	the	collateral	waterfall	(earned	premium	reserves,
Reg	114	Trust	loss	reserves,	segregated-account	structure,	retrocessional	tail	cover).
8
Compliance	&	Proof	of	Reserves
Institutional	LPs	are	KYC/AML-verified	off-chain;	verified	addresses	are	allowlisted	on-chain	for	subscription	and	redemption.	The	Bermuda	SAC	/	BVI	structure
provides	the	regulated	wrapper	for	the	underlying	reinsurance	activity.	A	Stellar	Info	File	(SEP-1	stellar.toml)	will	be	published	for	the	baUSD	asset	linking	to	third-
party	proof	of	backing,	the	independent	actuarial	review	plus	Reg	114	Trust	reserve	confirmation,	prior	to	mainnet.

10
Why	Stellar
Low,	predictable	fees	suited	to	frequent	NAV	updates	and	institutional	settlement.
Leading	tokenized-RWA	ecosystem	(Franklin	Templeton	BENJI,	Ondo	USDY,	WisdomTree);	reinsurance	premium	is	the	logical	next	RWA	yield	vertical,	yielding
more	than	tokenized	treasuries.
DeFindex	distribution	turns	baUSD	into	productive,	wallet-accessible	yield	rather	than	idle	TVL.
A	deep	set	of	composable	building	blocks	(Blend,	Soroswap,	Aquarius,	CCTP)	lets	baUSD	become	collateral,	liquidity,	and	cross-chain-fundable	over	time.
