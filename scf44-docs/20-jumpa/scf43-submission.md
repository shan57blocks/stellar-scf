# JUMPA - Automated Payment Platform (SCF #43)

- Status: Build, Prescreen Failed
- Budget requested: $79.7K ($79,650)
- Category: End-User Application

## Products & Services

"An automated payment platform designed to simplify cross border payment, stablecoin on-ramp/off-ramp while providing RwA yield opportunities."

Planned features:
- Chat interface ("Send $50", "Pay my Netflix", "Swap USDT to ETH") with no wallet connection needed
- Multi-language support (major African and international languages)
- Tokenized real-world assets (commercial paper, real estate fractions)
- Virtual cards (USD and EUR accounts)
- Shared wallets and bill splitting
- USSD interface for feature phones

Target markets: Francophone Africa (Cameroon, Benin, Senegal, Rwanda, Seychelles, Madagascar) and Southeast Asia (Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Philippines).

## How Stellar was to be used

- Settlement layer: all payments, transfers, swaps and ramps settle on Stellar
- Anchor Platform: SEP-24, SEP-10, SEP-12, SEP-1
- USDC on Stellar
- Soroban contracts: shared wallets, bill splitting, recurring payments, tokenized RWAs
- Gas abstraction: users never hold XLM
- Merchant payouts in local currency

## Traction (as claimed)

- Selected for SEVCP (129 of 1,200+ applicants); chosen by Superteam NG as a top product in Nigeria
- 300+ community members
- 87+ pilot users, 450+ test transactions on Stellar testnet, $3,000+ volume
- MOU with Switch (on/off-ramp provider); interest from 12 merchants (Nigeria, Kenya, Thailand)
