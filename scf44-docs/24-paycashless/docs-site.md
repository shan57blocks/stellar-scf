# Use cases

#### Accepting In-Store Transfers via POS

Merchants can offer customers the option to pay by bank transfer using a modern POS terminal with Paycashless.

#### Generating Payment Links for Social Orders

Businesses can quickly create and share payment links through WhatsApp or social media to collect payments remotely.

#### Dedicated Virtual Accounts for Merchants

eCommerce platforms can assign each seller a unique virtual account for simplified payment tracking and reconciliation.

#### Reliable, Branded Checkout Experience

Online brands can integrate a customizable checkout widget with high uptime and automatic bank failover to reduce drop-offs.
