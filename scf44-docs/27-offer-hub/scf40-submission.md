## Products & Services

-   **Payment-Escrow Marketplace:** Freelance contract system with non-custodial, milestone-based escrow. Payments are automatically locked and released as deliverables are completed, ensuring security for both clients and freelancers.
    
-   **Dispute Resolution Upgrade:** Fully on-chain dispute resolution with evidence locking and community-driven arbiter workflows. Increases transparency and removes delays or arbitrary decisions.
    
-   **Payouts & FX:** Multi-currency payouts with built-in automatic FX conversion. Users can receive funds in their preferred currency without relying on traditional banking intermediaries.
    
-   **Reputation NFTs:** Non-transferable credentials representing a verifiable record of completed work and ratings. Replace centralized reputation systems with transparent, blockchain-based proof.
    
-   **Fee Manager:** Monetization model based on a flat **2.5%** transaction fee, programmatically distributed between the platform and ecosystem contributors. Transparent, predictable, and free of hidden charges.
    
-   **Wallet Integrations:** One-click payments and connections through _invisible wallets_ and external wallet integrations. Accounts are created automatically upon login, removing friction and enabling instant access to the decentralized financial system.

## Success Criteria

Offer-Hub’s success will be reflected in its ability to attract and retain a strong initial base of freelancers and companies, making it easy and secure for both sides to find what they’re looking for. In the first few months, we aim to demonstrate real connections: projects posted, proposals submitted, and deals closed on the platform. It will be key that the first transactions are completed with satisfaction on both sides, building trust and driving organic recommendations.

We’ll measure success not just by the number of registered users, but by their activity and engagement—users returning, projects progressing, and work relationships repeating. The most meaningful impact will be seeing previously excluded freelancers gain access to international opportunities, while companies quickly and seamlessly meet their talent needs. If, after their first experiences, our users feel that their careers or businesses are advancing thanks to Offer-Hub, we’ll know the goal is being achieved.
