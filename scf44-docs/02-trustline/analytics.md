Pilot Success: Strategic Deployment with a Leading Fund

We will replicate this powerful, non-invasive approach on Stellar, enabling institutions to confidently adopt existing, battle-tested smart contracts while meeting their stringent control and insurance mandates. Furthermore, we are finalizing a second pilot with a high-profile, soon-to-be-launched stablecoin project.

Market Opportunity: Tapping a $17 Trillion Insurable Market


The market opportunity for financial services growth on-chain, catalyzed by institutional participation, is colossal. The market for growing insurance coverage is currently vast and untapped. Trustline addresses a double-sided market opportunity, with significant potential on the Stellar network.
* The Insurance Gap: Traditional financial services are built upon insurance. Currently, less than 1% of financial services on the blockchain are insured, a stark and unsustainable contrast to the 22% penetration observed in traditional finance.
* Projected Growth: The financial insurance market is on a trajectory for explosive growth:
   * Digital Asset Growth: A 40% Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) will propel digital financial assets from $600 billion to $17 trillion by 2036.
   * Insurance Penetration: A parallel increase in insurance penetration (from 1% to 22%) represents a formidable 22x multiplier.
With the accelerating pace of legislative support (e.g., GENIUS and CLARITY Acts), institutional investments in on-chain financial assets are surging. These smart contracts absolutely require customary, traditional-finance-grade insurance protections in the event of an unforeseen incident, a necessity Trustline is uniquely positioned to deliver.
